# 🕉️ About Bholenath Dham

**Bholenath Dham** is a sacred Shiva temple and devotional initiative dedicated to **Lord Shiva — Mahadev, Bholenath, Shankara and the Supreme Lord of Kailasa**.

<div align="center">

**🛕 Bholenath Dham**

**Digha, Chandpara**  
**North 24 Parganas**  
**West Bengal – 743245**  
**India**

</div>

The sacred journey began in **January 2025** with the acquisition of temple land and culminated in the **inauguration of the temple on 14 February 2026**.

The temple serves devotees through Puja, Aarti, Seva, Annadan, festivals, spiritual activities, religious learning and devotional gatherings.

### Devotion • Knowledge • Seva • Dharma • Community

# 🛠️ Development Guidelines

1. Preserve existing URL structures.
2. Preserve relative paths.
3. Preserve Hindi, English and Bengali language switching.
4. Do not remove existing Shiva Purana chapters.
5. Update internal links when moving pages.
6. Update `sitemap.xml` when adding indexable pages.
7. Keep canonical URLs and SEO metadata correct.
8. Test all supported languages.
9. Test mobile and desktop layouts.
10. Check browser console errors.
11. Optimize large images.
12. Maintain consistent navigation.
13. Check broken links after structural changes.
14. Verify sitemap entries after adding or removing pages.
15. Avoid unnecessary dependencies and keep the website lightweight.
