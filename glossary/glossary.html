❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Lord Shiva is one of the principal deities of Hinduism and is worshipped as the Supreme Lord in Shaivism. He is known as Mahadev, the destroyer of ignorance and the source of spiritual transformation.

Om Namah Shivaya is one of the most sacred mantras dedicated to Lord Shiva. It means "I bow to Shiva" and symbolizes surrender to the Divine.

Rudraksha is a sacred seed traditionally associated with Lord Shiva. It is commonly worn for spiritual protection, meditation and devotion.

A Jyotirlinga is a sacred manifestation of Lord Shiva represented as an infinite pillar of divine light. There are twelve famous Jyotirlingas in India.

Maha Shivaratri is the most important festival dedicated to Lord Shiva. Devotees observe fasting, prayer and night-long worship seeking the blessings of Mahadev.
